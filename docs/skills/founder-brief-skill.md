# Founder Brief Skill

## Purpose

Condense company chat, updates, and operating context into founder-ready briefs.

## What It Does

- Pulls decisions, risks, blockers, priorities, and open questions from noisy collaboration surfaces.
- Keeps the brief focused on what changed and what needs attention.
- Preserves unresolved context instead of forcing false certainty.
- Produces a concise handoff that can guide follow-up work.

## Why It Matters

Founder updates often live across chat, docs, calls, and half-finished notes. This skill turns that context into a useful operating brief without pretending the system knows more than it does.

## Outputs

- Founder summary
- Decisions made
- Open decisions
- Risks and blockers
- Recommended next actions
