# Decision Review Skill

## Purpose

Compare evidence, constraints, owners, and timing before recommending the next operating move.

## What It Does

- Identifies the constraint that matters most.
- Reviews available evidence and missing information.
- Maps the decision to owner, timing, and downstream impact.
- Produces a recommendation with uncertainty clearly marked.

## Why It Matters

AI output is weakest when it jumps straight to an answer. This skill slows the system down enough to review the decision shape before suggesting action.

## Outputs

- Decision summary
- Constraint map
- Evidence review
- Recommended next step
- Uncertainty and review notes
