# Source Review Skill

## Purpose

Turn source documents, links, notes, and research folders into grounded briefs with clear evidence boundaries.

## What It Does

- Summarizes source material without treating every detail as confirmed.
- Separates facts, interpretations, open questions, and missing evidence.
- Identifies claims that should not be made publicly.
- Produces a public-safe or internal-facing review brief depending on context.

## Why It Matters

Research work becomes useful only when the evidence boundary is visible. This skill keeps source review from becoming generic summary work by preserving what the source actually supports.

## Outputs

- Source brief
- Evidence list
- Unsupported claims
- Open questions
- Public-safe summary
